    Clarkston Community Center

    Can help point you to county housing supports

    Clarkston Cares Fund

    A local nonprofit focused on helping families stay in their homes and avoid homelessness. They provide emergency assistance, direct talks with landlords, and case support when someone is at risk of losing housing.

    House Choice Voucher

    Through the Georgia Department of Community Affairs, this program (often called Section 8) provides rental assistance to low-income individuals and families so rent stays affordable.
